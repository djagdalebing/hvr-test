1 Becoming a creator

Anyone with an HVN account can become a creator — there's no application and no waiting. Open Account Settings and choose Become a Creator. That's the whole process.

You'll get a public creator profile and access to the Creator Dashboard. Your profile is where viewers learn who you are: a display name, a bio, links to your site and socials, and your previous work.

2 Adding your work

From the Creator Dashboard, choose Upload new title. You'll need:

  • A title and cover image. The cover is what viewers see everywhere on the site — portrait artwork works best.
  • The video itself, either as a file upload or a link to YouTube or Vimeo. Uploaded files go straight to our storage, so large files don't time out.
  • A type — film, short, documentary, series, or proof of concept.

Everything else — synopsis, year, runtime, cast and crew, trailer — is optional, but titles with a synopsis and cast get watched more. You can add them later.

Series and multi-episode work

Choose Series as the type, and the video you upload becomes episode 1. After that, use Manage episodes on the series in your dashboard to add the rest — one at a time, as you release them. Pick an existing season or create a new one; episode numbers fill in automatically.

Episodes added to a series that's already live stay hidden until they're reviewed. The rest of the series keeps playing in the meantime — publishing episode 6 never takes episodes 1 to 5 offline.

3 Review

Every submission is reviewed by the HVN team before it goes live. You'll see the status on each title in your dashboard:

  • Pending review We've received it and haven't looked yet.
  • Approved It's live and viewers can watch it.
  • Rejected We can't publish it as submitted. Hover the status to see why.

A rejection isn't permanent. Fix what was flagged, and we'll look again. If the reason doesn't make sense to you, send us feedback and ask.

4 Your dashboard

The Creator Dashboard is where you manage everything:

  • Your titles — status of each, edit details, manage episodes, remove work.
  • Previous work / projects — a portfolio section on your public profile for work that doesn't live on HVN.
  • Earnings — views, eligible views and your current balance.
  • Edit profile — your public bio, links and display name.

Keeping your profile filled in matters more than it sounds. Viewers who finish something often go looking for who made it, and an empty profile is where that trail ends.

What counts as an eligible view

Not every click. A view earns when:

  • the viewer is signed in;
  • they watch at least {{ earnings.min_seconds }} seconds of actual playback — skipping ahead doesn't count, because we measure time played, not where the scrubber is;
  • it's the full title, not a trailer or clip;
  • the title is approved;
  • it's their first view of that title in {{ earnings.dedupe_hours }} hours;
  • the viewer isn't you, and isn't HVN staff.

Your dashboard shows total views and eligible views side by side, so you can always see the difference rather than wondering about it.

Getting paid

  • Your balance builds as people watch.
  • Once it reaches {{ money(earnings.min_payout) }}, you're due a payout.
  • Payouts are issued manually by the HVN team, and your dashboard shows the status of each.

The rate is recorded at the moment of each view. If HVN raises it later, everything you've already earned stays exactly as shown, and new views earn the new rate. Your past statements never change underneath you.
